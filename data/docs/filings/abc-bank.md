# ABC Bank counterparty brief

ABC Bank is a synthetic financial-sector counterparty used in the sample book. The brief is not a regulatory filing.

Sector: Financials. Country: US. Illustrative rating: A. The sample book maps that rating to a one-year default probability of 0.0012 and a recovery rate of 0.40.

The sample netting set contains one USD interest rate swap and one EURUSD forward. Collateral is daily cash. This document does not authorize a limit increase.
